From Concept to Shipped in 6 Steps

Our agile process ensures complete transparency, rapid iterations, and guaranteed on-time delivery.

01

Discovery & Strategy

We begin by understanding your business goals, target audience, brand aesthetic, and architectural requirements to chart an exact roadmap.

02

Planning & Architecture

We map the technical architecture, content strategy, user flow wireframes, and creative asset shot lists before writing code or filming.

Mubaris engineers clean, modular code with responsive layouts, while Rasal produces cinematic video cuts, AI video, and ad graphics.

06

Launch & Growth

We deploy to global edge CDNs, configure custom domains & SSL, and launch targeted ad campaigns with full performance tracking.
