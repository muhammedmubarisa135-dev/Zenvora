My Workflow

How I Work With Clients

A straightforward, collaborative process — from first conversation to finished product.

01

Discovery & Scoping

We start with a conversation. I ask about your goals, audience, visual preferences, and technical requirements. No obligation.

02

Design & Planning

I create wireframes, propose the structure, and map the user experience before writing a single line of code. Your sign-off before build begins.

03

Build & Review

I write clean, well-structured code and share progress for feedback. Revisions are part of the process — not an extra charge.

04

Launch & Handover

Deployment, domain configuration, and a clear handover so you understand your own website. Post-launch support as agreed.
